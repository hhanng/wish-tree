Wish Tree

Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Wish Tree is a small personal project that lets someone plant a Christmas tree and collect sealed wishes from friends. This page explains what it stores and why.

What is collected

If you leave a wish: the message you write, the ornament you pick, the name you type, the time you sent it, and any photo or video you choose to attach. The name and the attachment are optional — leave the name blank and the wish is anonymous. Wishes left on a tree found through the forest are always anonymous. No account is needed and nothing else about you is recorded.

If you own a tree: the email address or Google account you sign in with, your tree's name, its link name, and its opening date. Signing in with Google shares your email address and basic profile with Wish Tree. Nothing is read from your Google account beyond that, and nothing is ever written to it.

How it is used

Stored data is used only to run the site: to show wishes on the right tree on the right day, and to let a tree owner sign back in to their own tree. It is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, and it is not used to build a profile of you or to train any model.

Who can see a wish

Wishes, and any photos or videos attached to them, stay sealed until the tree's opening date. After that date only the tree's owner can read and view them. Please do not put passwords, financial details, or anything confidential in a wish.

The forest

Every tree appears in the public Wish Forest with its owner's name, its link, and how many wishes it holds. The wishes themselves are never shown there. To leave the forest, turn off “Show my tree in the forest” on your My tree page; your tree still counts toward the total but is no longer listed.

Where it is stored

Data is held in a Supabase project hosted in the United States, and the site itself is served by Vercel. Both receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type as part of serving a web page. Access to the database is restricted by row-level security rules.

Cookies

Wish Tree sets no advertising or analytics cookies. If you sign in as a tree owner, your browser stores a session token so you stay signed in; clearing your browser storage signs you out.

Keeping and deleting data

Wishes stay on a tree until the owner deletes the tree. You can ask for your wish, your tree, or your account to be deleted at any time by emailing the address below, and it will be removed.

Children

Wish Tree is not directed at children under 13 and no account is needed to leave a wish.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
